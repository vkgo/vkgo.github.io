> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Publish the ACM MM 2026 paper metadata across the website, news feed, and downloadable CV without exposing the private manuscript or adding paper links.

**Architecture:** Keep the website publication as a single BibTeX source of truth, add one conventional Jekyll news post, and extend the existing LaTeX CV publication renderer so entries may intentionally omit abstracts and URLs. Rebuild both Jekyll and the tracked downloadable CV artifact.

**Tech Stack:** Jekyll, jekyll-scholar/BibTeX, Liquid, LaTeX/XeLaTeX, shell content checks.

- Show the venue as `ACM MM 2026`.
- Use the exact supplied title and complete supplied author list.
- Show the paper on both Publications and the homepage selected-publications section.

- Add the paper to the downloadable CV.
- Do not copy or publish `/tmp/acmmm/1150_ChoreoPlan_Hybrid_Phrase_.pdf`.

### Task 2: Downloadable CV

**Files:**
- Modify: `cv_latex/wei_jin_huang_cv.tex`
- Modify: `assets/pdf/wei_jin_huang_cv.pdf`
- Test: `/tmp/codex-0724-acm-mm-paper/verify_content.sh`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: the existing six-argument `\pub` command.
- Produces: conditional title-link and abstract rendering plus a new unlinked ACM MM publication entry.

- [ ] **Step 1: Allow a publication to omit a URL and abstract**

Replace the unconditional title and abstract rendering inside `\pub` with:

```tex
    \if\relax\detokenize{#6}\relax
      {\bfseries\color{ink} #2}\par
    \else
      \href{#6}{\bfseries\color{ink} #2}\par
    \fi
    {\footnotesize\color{muted}#3}\par
    \vspace{0.5pt}#4\par
    \if\relax\detokenize{#5}\relax\else
      \vspace{1pt}{\color{ink}{\bfseries\color{primary}Abstract:} #5}\par
    \fi
```

- [ ] **Step 2: Add and renumber the publications**
